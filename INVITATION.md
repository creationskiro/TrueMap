# 🚀 Invitation to Contribute: Build the Future of Indoor Navigation

Hey Developers, Creators, and Spatial Computing Enthusiasts!

We are the core team behind **TrueMap**, and we are on a mission to map the "99% of unmapped indoor spaces" globally.

Have you ever been lost in a massive airport, a multi-story hospital, or a sprawling university campus because Google Maps or Apple Maps dropped you at the front door? **We are fixing that, and we need your help.**

We believe that spatial data should not be locked behind expensive B2B enterprise contracts. Instead, we are building a community-driven, open-source 3D mapping engine. Think of it as **Waze, but for indoor 3D spaces.**

### We Need Mobile Developers (React Native / Expo)
Our core web engine and API architecture (built on Next.js 15 and Three.js) are already open-sourced and live. But to truly democratize indoor mapping, we need to put the scanning technology directly into people's hands.

We have just scaffolded the initial `truemap-mobile` repository using **React Native (Expo)**.

We are looking for iOS and Android developers to help us build:
1. **The AR Scanner**: Integrating camera APIs to capture spatial data and generate 3D point clouds.
2. **The 3D Viewer**: Implementing `react-three-fiber` on mobile to render our beautiful 3D cities and blueprints directly on the phone.
3. **The Community Hub**: Building the Reddit-style Q&A system where users can ask for directions and receive visual 3D guides.

### Why Contribute to TrueMap?
- **Impact**: You are directly solving a massive real-world problem.
- **Cutting-Edge Tech**: You will be working at the intersection of Spatial Computing, AR, React Native, and AI.
- **Open-Source Glory**: Become a core maintainer of a project designed to disrupt the mapping industry.

### How to Join Us
If you want to help build the TrueMap mobile app:
1. Check out the web repository at [https://github.com/creationskiro/TrueMap](https://github.com/creationskiro/TrueMap).
2. Look at the `truemap-mobile` folder for the Expo initialization.
3. Check the open issues tagged with `mobile`, `good first issue`, or `react-native`.
4. Fork, Clone, and submit a PR!

Let's build the ultimate indoor mapping platform together.
